Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters. This page explains how information may be handled when you use the FINOVA website and related services.


Effective date: 6 October 2026

1. Introduction

FINOVA ("FINOVA", "we", "us", or "our") is a developing financial technology project focused on helping users understand and organise personal finance.


This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information handled through the FINOVA website and its current features.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the feature you use, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address or information contained in a question submitted to FINOVA.


You should avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, card numbers, authentication codes or other highly sensitive information into public or demonstration features.

3. Ask FINOVA

The current Ask FINOVA experience operates locally within the website. Questions may be processed by the local finance-answer engine included in the page.


In this current version, conversations may be stored in your browser's local storage so that they can remain available on the same device.

4. Local Storage

FINOVA may use browser local storage for functionality such as remembering an Ask FINOVA conversation.


Local storage is controlled by your browser and remains on your device unless browser data is cleared or the website changes how the feature works.

5. How Information May Be Used

  • To provide and improve FINOVA features.
  • To respond to questions or requests you voluntarily submit.
  • To improve the website experience.
  • To understand technical problems and improve functionality.

6. Information We Do Not Ask You To Submit

FINOVA does not need your banking password, debit or credit card PIN, OTP, authentication code or similar credentials for the basic website experience.

7. Third-Party Services

The website may eventually integrate third-party services to provide specific functionality. If such services are introduced, their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

8. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information associated with FINOVA. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

FINOVA is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information unnecessarily, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices

You can clear browser-stored FINOVA information through your browser's storage or site-data controls. You can also choose not to submit unnecessary personal information through optional features.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FINOVA develops new features, integrations or services. The latest version will be made available on this page together with an updated effective date.
